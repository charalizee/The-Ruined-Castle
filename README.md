# The Ruined Castle

You are an explorer who loves discovering abandoned places. One day, you hear rumors about an old ruined castle that supposedly holds a legendary treasure known as **the Azure Latch**.

Legend says that whoever finds the Azure Latch will gain riches beyond their wildest dreams. However, nobody who has searched for it has ever managed to find it.

After a long journey, you finally arrive at the ruined castle. Its walls are falling apart, and nobody seems to have been there for years.

Determined to find the treasure, you step inside the castle and begin your adventure.

**Will you be the first to uncover the secrets of the ruined castle and find the Azure Latch?**
